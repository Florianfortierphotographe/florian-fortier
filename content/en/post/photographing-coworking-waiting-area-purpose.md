A pool table readily attracts attention in an interior photograph. Its surface, colour and size can become the first information the viewer notices. When it stands in a coworking waiting area, the framing also needs to explain where people can sit and how the equipment fits into the space.

That reading is useful when presenting a commercial building. It shows the options available while waiting through the way they are arranged. In my [commercial property photography](/en/retail-commercial/), I aim to make these relationships apparent: the equipment that catches the eye, the available seating and the areas through which people move.

## Place the waiting area within the presentation of the building

For this assignment, the client asked me to photograph the different spaces within the coworking premises. The images were intended to support the marketing of the building. This photograph presented its waiting area, including the pool table, the décor and the opportunities to sit down.

The space is generally used by clients or people working in the coworking premises, particularly when waiting for an appointment. It therefore has a specific function within the building. The photograph needed to provide information about what is available during that waiting time.

My intention was to show the presence of the pool table, the overall décor and the circulation space. I also gave the seating a place in the background to show that people could sit down and potentially work on a laptop. Together, these elements describe several ways of using the area.

This view belongs to an assignment covering the different professional spaces. I discuss that broader approach in my article on [photographing coworking and shared workspaces](/en/post/coworking-shared-spaces-photographing-workspaces/). Here, the subject is how a waiting area can be understood around a particularly prominent piece of equipment.

## Build the framing around the pool table

I primarily framed the photograph around the pool table to highlight it. It occupies much of the foreground: the green playing surface, edges and legs make it immediately identifiable. The table top is visible, along with the balls arranged on it.

That choice provides a starting point for reading the image. The pool table gives concrete information about an amenity within this waiting area. Its presence is strong enough for the viewer to notice it before examining the rest of the room.

The space retained around it matters just as much. A frame limited to the playing surface would mainly show the game. Here, the seating behind it is visible too, along with part of the floor towards the lift on the right. Those details place the pool table within a professional setting where it is one of the available amenities.

I do not think there were any particular technical constraints for this image. The decisive choice I remember is the framing around the pool table. The aim was to give it a strong presence while retaining the elements that explain the space’s purpose. This relationship between the planes connects with my approach to [architectural composition](/en/post/secrets-good-composition-architecture-photography/).

## Give the background seating an explanatory role

The seating provides different information from the pool table. It indicates the possibility of waiting seated, with tables in front. Its presence helps explain that the area can also accommodate time spent sitting down, potentially using a laptop.

I positioned the seating in the background of the image to retain that information within the frame. It does not need to occupy as much space as the pool table to contribute to the viewer’s understanding. A few recognisable reference points can show another way of using the area.

In the photograph, the light-coloured tables stand out against the darker seats. That difference makes their relationship readable despite the substantial foreground. The eye can therefore move from the pool table to a place where people can settle down.

The possibility of working on a laptop belongs here to a period of waiting. The seats and tables provide reference points for imagining a temporary place to sit and work. I retain that reading in the background, where it complements the more prominent pool table.

## Retain reference points for understanding circulation

My intention also included showing the circulation space. In a waiting area, equipment and seating occupy part of a building through which people continue to move. The image becomes more informative when it retains clues to that arrangement.

On the right, the lift provides an identifiable reference point. The visible floor in front of it locates the pool table in relation to that access. Further back, the openings between furniture also give information about the room’s depth and the position of its different elements.

The central column occupies a substantial part of the photograph. It visually separates some of the seating from the cupboards behind it. On either side, the visible sections of the background help locate the different elements. The viewer therefore finds information about the room to the left and right of the column.

The view makes it possible to identify access points and areas for movement near the pool table. These reference points support the presentation of the equipment in its immediate surroundings. They help explain how the waiting area sits within a part of the building that also accommodates circulation.

## Show the décor at different distances

I also wanted to present the overall décor. The patterned rug occupies the floor beneath the pool table, while the graphic surround of the lift appears on the right. In the background, the large illuminated wall panel sits above the seating. These elements distribute the décor across different parts of the image.

The pool table contributes to that reading through its green surface and dark structure. The framing therefore brings the leisure amenity together with the features that characterise its surroundings. The game is identifiable while the setting in which it is offered remains visible.

The background has an important role here: it supplies information that a tighter frame would leave out. The décor accompanies the seating, access points and pool table. Their relationship within this photograph presents the waiting area progressively, from the foreground towards the rest of the room.

## Select a principal image for this space

This is the main image of the area. I do not remember making other photographs that were selected. This view brings together, within one frame, the different aspects of the space I wanted to present during the assignment.

It gathers the information I aimed to show: the pool table as a point of attention, seating in the background, the décor and circulation reference points. For an assignment supporting the marketing of the building, that combination provides a concrete view of the space available to clients and the people working there.
